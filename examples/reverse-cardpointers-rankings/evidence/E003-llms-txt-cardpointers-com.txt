# CardPointers

> CardPointers is the #1 app for maximizing credit card rewards. It tells you exactly which card to use for every purchase and tracks thousands of card-linked offers so you never miss cashback or points.

## Key Info

- 5,000+ credit cards supported across 900+ banks in 258 countries
- Available on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac, Android, Vision Pro, and browser extensions (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
- Free tier available; CardPointers+ (Pro) unlocks unlimited cards, offers, and advanced features
